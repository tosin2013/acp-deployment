# Tutorials

Tutorials are **learning-oriented**. They take you through a practical experience with ACP deployment, building your skill and confidence. Follow along in your own environment — every step is something you do, not just read.

**Start here if:** You are new to ACP deployment or want to build a solid understanding by working through a complete example.

---

## Available tutorials

### [Deploy your first ACP cluster on IBM Cloud KVM](deploy-acp-on-ibm-cloud-kvm.md)
**What you build:** A 3-node OpenShift cluster on KVM VMs, with ODF storage, ZeroSSL TLS, and all platform services.
**Time:** 3–4 hours (mostly unattended).
**Skill built:** End-to-end understanding of the 13-step deployment pipeline.

### [Explore ODF persistent storage](explore-odf-storage.md)
**What you build:** PersistentVolumeClaims using Ceph block and file storage; data persistence verified across pod restarts.
**Time:** 30 minutes.
**Skill built:** How Kubernetes storage works on ODF; practical differences between RWO and RWX volumes.
